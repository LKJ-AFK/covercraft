Software Engineer Cover Letter Examples (2026)

Engineers are hired on shipped work. This letter shows the pattern — a feature you owned, metrics that moved, and a first-sprint plan that fits how teams actually work.

Full example · copy & customize Dear Hiring Manager, At FinTech Labs, I owned the payment retry service that cut failed transactions by 22% and reduced support tickets about the payment issue by a third. I shipped it in eight weeks with two other engineers, wrote the integration tests myself, and documented the system well enough that the team stopped pinging me about it within a month. The backend role at your company matches what I do best: owning a service end to end in TypeScript and Go, keeping latency budgets honest, and writing code that the next engineer can pick up. At FinTech Labs I also cut our CI build time from 14 minutes to 4 by parallelizing the test suite, and I led the migration of three services to a new queueing system without a single failed deploy. In my first sprint, I would take one small service, trace it from request to response, and ship a low-risk improvement with tests — then review the backlog with the team and propose the next two items I believe we should pick up. Sincerely, Alex Morgan
Replace every number with your own real results before sending — recruiters check. Never copy facts you can't back up.

Why this letter works

  • Shipped work, measured. "Failed transactions down 22% and tickets down a third" is what engineering managers hire on. — Open with a shipped outcome, not your stack list.
  • It proves ownership and hygiene. Integration tests, documentation the team stopped pinging about, CI from 14 to 4 minutes — these are the unglamorous wins teams feel. — Show maintainability, not just features.
  • Migration without failed deploys. "Three services, zero failed deploys" is the reliability signal senior teams screen for. — Include one operational proof point.
  • A first sprint that fits. "Trace one service, ship a low-risk improvement with tests" respects how teams work — no grand rearchitecture on day one. — Propose a small, testable first contribution.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a software engineer cover letter be?

160-200 words. Recruiters and hiring managers skim for shipped work; one feature and one operational win beat a paragraph of tech keywords.

Do I need to list every language and framework?

No. Name the ones the role uses and show them in action. A list without context reads as noise.

I am a junior engineer with one small project. What do I write?

Quantify it: users, latency, uptime, or bugs fixed. A small project with a measured result outranks a big one with none.
