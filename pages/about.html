Why I built CoverCraft

Short version: I got tired of AI writing tools that invent experience you never had — and getting caught on it in real interviews.

I'm an independent developer. CoverCraft started as a frustration: every AI letter generator I tried produced the same polished, empty paragraphs — "leveraged synergies", "thrilled to bring my passion" — and, worse, made up achievements that weren't mine. A recruiter who reads a thousand letters a week can smell that in one sentence. So can the ATS filter.

The one rule

CoverCraft has a single hard rule: never invent a fact. Every number, role and achievement in your letter has to come from what you actually typed. If you didn't say you cut costs by 30%, the letter won't say it either. When the model needs a number to make a sentence work and you haven't given one, it writes the placeholder clearly so you can fill in the real figure — it never guesses one for you.

How it actually works

  • You fill in your real details: job title, company, your skills, what you actually did. Paste the job description for a tighter match.
  • Every letter is written from your facts only, then checked like a hiring manager would read it — clichés, zero-information openers and vague filler are filtered out automatically.
  • You get a 0–100 score before you send it, plus three angles to pick the strongest.

What we will never do

  • We never store your resume, job description or letters on our servers — your content goes to the AI provider to generate the result, then stays with you in your browser.
  • We never sell your data. We never run ad trackers on this site. We don't even collect your email.
  • Payments are one-time and processed by Creem — no subscription, no recurring charges, no card data on our side.

Why $20

One-time, lifetime, unlimited letters. No monthly meter. If you're applying to one dream role, it's one cup of coffee per version of the letter you'll send. If you're mass-applying, it pays for itself by the second day. And it keeps this tool free for people who can't pay.
