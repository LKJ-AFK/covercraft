Marketing Manager Cover Letter Examples (2026)

Marketing managers are hired on growth curves. This letter shows the pattern — a campaign you owned, revenue it moved, and a first-quarter plan that fits the role.

Full example · copy & customize Dear Hiring Manager, As Marketing Manager at GreenHaus Foods, I grew e-commerce revenue from $180,000 to $340,000 in twelve months by rebuilding our paid search structure and launching a customer-retention email program. Paid CAC dropped 31%, and repeat purchases became a third of revenue by the end of the year. The marketing lead role at your company needs that blend of strategy and ownership. At GreenHaus I managed a $50,000 monthly budget across Google, Meta, and email, led a team of four, and ran the A/B testing calendar that lifted conversion 18%. I also worked with product on packaging and pricing, which taught me that marketing works best when it is in the room when decisions are made. In the first quarter with your team, I would audit the current funnel, map every campaign to a metric, and cut the two lowest performers within 30 days — then rebuild the winners with a testing plan for the rest of the quarter. Sincerely, Alex Morgan
Replace every number with your own real results before sending — recruiters check. Never copy facts you can't back up.

Why this letter works

  • Revenue in the first sentence. "$180,000 to $340,000 in twelve months" is the growth story a hiring manager wants to hear. — Open with revenue or pipeline, not a mission statement.
  • CAC as proof of efficiency. "Paid CAC down 31%" shows you do not just spend — you spend well. — Add one efficiency metric next to the growth number.
  • It shows leadership and cross-function work. A team of four and "in the room when decisions are made" signals a manager, not a solo contributor. — Prove you lead people and influence other functions.
  • A first quarter with a decision. "Cut the two lowest performers within 30 days" shows you will make the uncomfortable calls. — Commit to an early cut or a bold test.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a marketing manager cover letter be?

180-220 words. Hiring managers skim for growth numbers; two metrics and a quarter plan are enough.

Should I mention budget size?

Yes — a $50,000 monthly budget calibrates the role's scale and proves you have run real money.

What if I have not had a manager title?

Title matters less than scope. If you owned a campaign, a budget, or a channel end to end, name it and its result.
