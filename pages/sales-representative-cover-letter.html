Sales Representative Cover Letter Examples (2026)

Sales hiring is about numbers — quota, pipeline, close rate. This letter shows how to prove you can hit both the number and the relationship.

Full example · copy & customize Dear Hiring Manager, At Summit Office Solutions, I exceeded my annual quota by 118% last year and grew my territory revenue from $420,000 to $610,000 by focusing on repeat business and expanding two underdeveloped verticals. My close rate on qualified opportunities was 31%, and my average deal size grew 24% by moving from product pitches to value-based proposals. The sales representative role at your company runs on that same pair of numbers: quota attainment and pipeline discipline. At Summit I managed a pipeline of 40-50 active opportunities in Salesforce, ran discovery calls that averaged 35 minutes because I talked less and listened more, and cut my sales cycle from 90 to 60 days by aligning proposals to each buyer's budget process. I also won two consecutive President's Club trips by ranking top three in my region. In my first 90 days with your team, I would learn your product and ideal customer profile cold, map my first 50 target accounts, and book 30 qualified discovery meetings — then ask to be reviewed against that number at the end of the quarter. Sincerely, Alex Morgan
Replace every number with your own real results before sending — recruiters check. Never copy facts you can't back up.

Why this letter works

  • Quota in the first sentence. "118% of quota, territory from $420K to $610K" is the number sales managers screen for. — Open with attainment, not your pitch style.
  • It proves pipeline discipline. "40-50 active opportunities in Salesforce" and a 35-minute discovery average show you run a process, not just charm. — Include pipeline hygiene alongside the close rate.
  • It quantifies how you sell. "Close rate 31%, cycle from 90 to 60 days" — method with proof. — Show the mechanics behind the numbers.
  • A 90-day plan with a number to be judged on. "30 qualified discovery meetings, reviewed against that number" — you invite accountability. — Give them a number they can grade you on.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a sales cover letter be?

170-200 words. Sales managers skim for quota and pipeline; two attainment numbers and one process proof are enough.

Should I include every award?

Only with context. "Two President's Club trips, top three in region" supports the numbers; a list of certificates without results reads as filler.

What if I am new to sales?

Use any role where you influenced revenue: upsells in retail, renewal rates you supported, or deals you helped close. Numbers matter more than the title.
