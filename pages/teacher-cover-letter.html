Teacher Cover Letter Examples (2026)

Schools hire teachers who can point to student outcomes. This letter shows the pattern — measurable growth, a classroom routine that works, and what you would do in the first month.

Full example · copy & customize Dear Hiring Committee, As a fourth-grade teacher at Lincoln Elementary, I raised my class's reading proficiency from 58% to 76% over one school year by introducing daily 20-minute guided reading groups and a progress tracker students updated themselves. The tracker did more than measure growth — it gave students ownership, and discipline problems dropped because every child could see their own progress. The teaching position at your school asks for exactly that combination: strong literacy instruction and a classroom where students know the routine. At Lincoln I also led the grade-level math block, co-wrote our quarterly assessments, and mentored a first-year teacher through her first parent-teacher conferences. I plan my lessons a week ahead and send families a Monday note, which cut parent concerns by about a third in my first year. In my first month at your school, I would observe the grade-level team for a week, learn your literacy curriculum, and run a reading assessment within the first ten days so we both know the starting point. I would bring my lesson plans and student data to an interview whenever it suits you. Sincerely, Alex Morgan
Replace every number with your own real results before sending — recruiters check. Never copy facts you can't back up.

Why this letter works

  • Student outcomes first. "58% to 76% reading proficiency" is the number a committee remembers. — Open with student growth, not your teaching philosophy.
  • It proves classroom management. The self-updated tracker shows behavior improved because students owned their progress — management without a lecture about discipline. — Tie behavior to a system, not to your personality.
  • It names real duties. Math block, assessments, mentoring, family notes — this is a teacher who has run a classroom. — List responsibilities, not adjectives.
  • A measurable first month. "A reading assessment within the first ten days" shows you start by gathering data, not by guessing. — Start your plan with an assessment, not an introduction.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a teacher cover letter be?

180-220 words, three paragraphs. Principals read dozens; lead with one student outcome and one proof of classroom management.

Do I need a cover letter if I have a teaching license?

Yes. The license proves credentials; the letter proves results. Committees use it to rank candidates with identical licenses.

I am a new teacher with no classroom of my own. What do I write?

Use student teaching: the class size, the subject, one measurable outcome, and the routine you managed. Numbers beat job titles here too.
