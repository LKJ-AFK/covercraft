          FAQ
          
  
          
    
          Is CoverCraft really free?
          Yes. You get 1 free cover letter every day without signing up. Pro is a $20.00 one-time purchase for unlimited letters, A/B versions and all tones.
          
    
          How is the score calculated?
          Each letter is scored 0-100 for job match, ATS-friendliness, persuasion strength and length (160-240 words is the sweet spot). Scores are honest — they tell you when to strengthen the letter.
          
    
          Will my letter sound like a robot wrote it?
          No. The AI writes in a natural first-person voice using your actual skills and experience, not boilerplate. Banned clichés like "thrilled" and "passionate" are filtered out automatically.
          
    
          Can I use the letters for real applications?
          Absolutely. Every letter is generated for you and yours to use. We recommend reviewing and personalizing before sending — it is your application after all.
          
    
          What are the subject lines for?
          When applying by email, the subject line is the first thing a recruiter reads. You get three options with the role and company name — pick one and paste.
          
    
          Why does it read like a person wrote it?
          Two guardrails. First, the AI never invents facts — every number, role and achievement stays exactly as you wrote it. Second, a quality gate filters out what screams "AI": banned clichés ("thrilled", "passionate", "eager"), zero-information openers and template sentences get flagged and rewritten before you ever see them.
          
    
          What is interview prep?
          After your letter is diagnosed (or generated), Pro users can generate the 5 questions a recruiter will most likely ask about this application — each with why it's asked and 2-3 answer points grounded in your real facts. It turns one polished letter into a prepared interview.
          
    
          What is the email version?
          Many applications ask you to paste your cover letter into the email body instead of attaching a file. The Pro email version condenses your letter into a 100-150 word, ready-to-paste email body with the greeting included.
