Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026. This policy explains what CoverCraft collects, what we never store, and how your data is used when you use our AI cover letter generator.

1. What we collect

Text you submit. When you generate a letter, your role details, skills, company notes, job description and existing letter text are sent to our AI provider (DeepSeek) solely to produce your result. We do not store your submitted content on our servers.

Usage data on your device. Your free-tier usage count, activation status, and your most recent letters are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your browser.

Anonymous statistics. When a report is generated, we may record anonymous keyword statistics (for example, which skills appear in job descriptions) to improve our products. These statistics contain no names, contact details, or identifying information.

2. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not store your resume, job description, or generated letters on our servers.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party ad cookies on this site.
  • We do not send marketing email to you — we do not collect your email at all.

3. Payments

Purchases are processed by our payment providers (Creem and Payoneer). We never see or store your card details. Payments are one-time purchases of an activation key; no subscription or recurring billing is used.

4. Third-party services

CoverCraft runs on Cloudflare (hosting and network). AI generation is provided by DeepSeek. Each of these providers processes only the minimum data needed to operate the service and is subject to its own privacy policy.

5. Your choices

You can delete everything we know about you by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (local storage). Because we hold no account for you, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@ersi2026.top.

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
